Frequently asked <em>questions</em>
Can't find an answer? Ask us.
What exactly will I do?
You talk by video with men from Europe through a ready-made account: you get acquainted, keep the conversation interesting and try to keep the other person talking for longer. There are no other duties. The service connects you with people by itself.
Do I have to show explicit content?
No. Explicit content is prohibited, and an account is blocked for it.
How long is a shift?
A shift lasts 7–8 hours. It can be split into several parts during the day.
Can I choose my days off?
Yes. You choose two days off per week yourself; they do not have to be Saturday and Sunday.
How much do you pay per shift?
<span data-shift="">$30</span> is guaranteed pay for a fully worked 7–8 hour shift; it does not depend on the number of conversations. There are five shifts a week. For an extra hour of work on a day off you are paid <span data-extra="">$9</span>.
When and how is money paid?
Payouts arrive on Mondays to a bank card or in cryptocurrency, whichever suits you.
Do I need English?
No, the chat has an automatic translator. But you should be ready to talk with people from a different language environment.
Do I have to pay to register?
No. Registration and starting work are free, and no payment is required to create an account.
What do I need to work?
You must be 18 or older, have a phone or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection. No experience is needed. The project is aimed mainly at women from Russia.
What happens after I submit an application?
Telegram opens with the text of your application ready. After you send the message, the manager will write to you and invite you to an interview, where the terms are explained and your questions answered. Then an application to create your work account is submitted, and once the platform confirms it you can start preparing for work.
What if I don't like the work?
You decide whether to start work yourself, once you have seen the terms. The rules for ending cooperation are not described on the site, so ask about them before you start: that way you will know the terms in advance.

How we handle your data
What data we receive
Only what you enter in the application yourself: name, age, contact (Telegram or phone), city or time zone, convenient time and comment.
Why it is needed
Only so that we can contact you, explain the terms and answer your questions. We do not use it for advertising.
Where the data goes and what happens in Telegram
The website form does not send data to a server. It builds the application text in your browser and opens Telegram, where you press “Send” yourself. Until you send the message, nobody receives anything. After sending, the message is stored in the Telegram conversation and Telegram's rules apply to it.
Who has access
The manager to whom you send the message in Telegram.
How long data is kept
While the conversation about your application continues. If you decide not to continue, ask for the conversation and the application data to be deleted.
How to request deletion
Write to the manager in Telegram. We will delete the application data and the conversation on our side; messages in your Telegram and copies that Telegram keeps under its own rules do not depend on us.
